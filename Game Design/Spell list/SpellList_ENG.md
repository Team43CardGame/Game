## The game is balanced for 5–6 players, but there's nothing stopping you from playing with three  
## **V_3 Spells from 2 Cards**  

----

- R + R — Chosen opponent draws a card from the deck  

- R + Y — Both neighbors draw a card from the deck  

- R + B — Chosen player draws an additional card each time they should draw. The effect lasts 1 round or until they draw 2 extra cards  

- Y + R — Chosen player discards a card from their spell (the attacking player chooses the card), or draws a card from the deck if the spell is empty  

- Y + Y — Chosen opponent skips their turn  

- Y + B — Steal a card from an enemy’s spell, that card becomes the start of a new spell  

- B + R — Reflection — if someone tries to use a spell on the caster, the effect is reflected but not blocked. Lasts until the next turn  

- B + Y — Dispel on the caster, removes debuffs  

- B + B — Shield on a player for 2 rounds (can block up to 2 spells)  

## **V_3 Spells from 3 Cards**  

----

- R + R + R — Chosen player draws 2 cards  

- R + R + Y — Ignition – The player draws 1 card, then every 2 rounds for 4 rounds (up to 3 cards total)  

- R + R + B — Chosen player will draw 1 card each time they become the target of a spell. Effect lasts 1 round  

- R + Y + R — Random player draws as many cards as they have in their spell  

- R + Y + B — Return a random player’s spell to their hand  

- R + Y + Y — Chosen player draws 2 cards, and the next 2 players (skipping the caster) draw 1 card each  

- R + B + R — Chosen player draws 2 additional cards each time they should draw. Effect lasts 1 round or until they draw 4 extra cards (triggers twice)  

- R + B + Y — Random 3-card spell (If the target is an enemy, they are chosen randomly as well)  

- R + B + B — Choose a player to draw a card; also removes all buffs from the target. Even shields are removed, but reflection still works against this spell  

- Y + R + R — If within the next 3 turns the target skips a turn for any reason, they skip an additional turn and draw 2 cards  

- Y + R + Y — Choose a player; a random card from their spell returns to hand, and all players discard 1 random card from their spell (it simply disappears)  

- Y + R + B — Replaces all cards in all players’ hands with new ones (all discard and draw the same number from the deck)  

- Y + Y + R — Chosen player draws a card and skips their turn  

- Y + Y + Y — Chosen player and the next in turn order, skipping the caster, skip 2 turns each  

- Y + Y + B — Chosen player and their neighbors skip 1 turn each  

- Y + B + R — Steal the entire spell from an enemy; it becomes the caster's spell. The victim draws a card  

- Y + B + Y — Destroys all shields, of all types and powers, on all players (Reflection is not a shield)  

- Y + B + B — Buff that allows the caster to take an extra turn whenever they become the target of a spell. Lasts for 4 rounds or 2 activations  

- B + R + R — Random player draws 2 cards and skips a turn  

- B + R + Y — Replaces a chosen player's spell with a random one (same number of cards). They draw a card  

- B + R + B — Absolute Reflection — if someone uses a spell on the caster, it is both reflected and blocked. Lasts 2 rounds or until the first reflection  

- B + Y + R — Reverses turn order, caster starts the new round  

- B + Y + Y — Dispels all effects from all players. For each removed effect, a player draws a card (except the caster). Blocked by shields  

- B + Y + B — Buff that lets a player take 2 actions per turn, but they draw a card for each extra action. Lasts until they become the target of a spell (does not disappear if they have a shield). Also ends if the player takes only 1 action during their turn  

- B + B + R — Immunity to all spells that do not directly target the caster (includes all spells that affect multiple players, random targets, neighbors, next player, etc.) for 3 rounds  

- B + B + Y — Allows the caster to take a second consecutive turn and also grants a 1-turn shield that blocks 1 spell  

- B + B + B — Shield on a player for 4 rounds, can block up to 3 spells
